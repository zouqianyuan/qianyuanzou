Background and Research Interests

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Immersive Design Group at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), working on immersive interaction design and extended reality systems. I recently completed my PhD in Design at the Empathic Computing Lab at the University of Auckland, where my research focused on asymmetric input devices for 3D sketching and object editing in virtual reality.

My research spans human-computer interaction, extended reality, immersive design workflows, and accessibility in emerging technologies. I am particularly interested in how interaction techniques, input devices, and visual systems shape creative work in immersive environments, and how these systems can be designed to be more inclusive and expressive.

Across research, design, and visual practice, I explore how immersive systems can support creative work, inclusive interaction, and future mixed reality experiences. Beyond research, I maintain an active photography practice, which informs my thinking about framing, materiality, spatial attention, and how we perceive and represent physical and digital spaces.
